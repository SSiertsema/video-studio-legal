Video Studio

Video Studio is a private, self-hosted tool that renders short educational videos (for example the Fitness Factcheck series) and publishes them to the creator's own channels on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

It runs only on the creator's own computer. It is not a public service, has no user accounts and is not offered to third parties.
